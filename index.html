Tools

Free tools from the workflow

Both are single HTML files that run entirely in your browser. Nothing to install, no sign-up.

Character Sheet Builder

Describe your character in a simple form and get the two Stage 3 prompts for Nano Banana Pro, ready to copy and paste.

  • Portrait prompt (4:5). Generate this first and get the face right.
  • Three-panel character sheet prompt (16:9). Chest-up portrait, full-length front view and full-length rear view.

The workflow

Seven stages, idea to finished film

The same process every time, for cinematic shorts and AI persona content alike. Lock the character first, the location second, and describe only what a camera can see.

  1. 01

    Idea

    Five plain-language questions: what happens, who, where, tone, and the feeling at the end.

    The brief
  2. 02

    Script

    Physical actions in [brackets] inline with dialogue, each segment under 8 seconds.

    Claude / ChatGPT
  3. 03

    Images

    Portrait, three-panel character sheet and location plate, generated in a fixed order.

    Nano Banana Pro
  4. 04

    Prompts

    Block prompts with byte-identical Style DNA, timestamps and @image references.

    Claude / ChatGPT
  5. 05

    Video

    Generated block by block at 1080p, carrying each last frame forward for continuity.

    Seedance 2.5
  6. 06

    Edit

    Trim artifacts, cut on action, grade, and lay music under the native audio.

    CapCut / After Effects
  7. 07

    Finished

    Exported at 1080p or higher: 16:9 for YouTube, 9:16 for vertical platforms.

    Delivered
