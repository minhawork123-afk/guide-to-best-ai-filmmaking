## What Makes This Example Useful

- Built entirely from written descriptions — no real person's photos used
- Demonstrates the full pipeline from brief to finished video
- Shows how to handle multiple locations in one continuous narrative (apartment → gym)
- Demonstrates product reference integration (@Image2)
- Shows how to write UGC-style handheld camera feel in a Seedance prompt

---

## Pipeline at a Glance

```
BRIEF         Low-energy fitness persona reviewing protein powder
              Locations: apartment kitchen (pre-dawn) + industrial gym

SCRIPT        Hook → action shot → out the door → arrival (Block A)
              Chalk and load → the set → proof → verdict → close (Block B)

IMAGES        Tess three-panel sheet (Nano Banana Pro, from description)
              LODE product reference (four-panel, white background)
              Kitchen plate (tungsten vs cold blue window light)
              Gym wide establishing plate
              Gym filming bay plate (hard diagonal window light)

VIDEO         Seedance 2.5, omni_reference, 9:16, 1080p, 30s per block
              Block A: 0:00–0:15
              Block B: 0:15–0:30

EDIT          CapCut — blocks assembled, native audio kept, no music
```

---

## Key Decisions — Why They Were Made

**No music.** UGC authenticity lives in silence and room tone. Music signals "ad."

**Phone-shot look, not cinematic.** The whole film is framed as self-recorded content.
Hard directional daylight in the gym functions as a practical key —
the cinematography is real, it just looks like someone propped their phone well.

**Low energy performance.** Talking to her phone like she's talking to a friend.
No enthusiasm, no smiling at the lens. The more understated, the more credible.

**Two location plates, one gym.** Plate A is the wide establishing shot for her arrival.
Plate B is the filming bay corner where she actually records — the window daylight
doing real work as a key light.

**Product reference as @Image2.** The LODE tub and shaker get their own reference image
so Seedance can match the product exactly in close-up shots without reinventing it.
